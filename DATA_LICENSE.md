# Data licence

Tychonides dedicates its original catalogue contributions, structured numerical
transcriptions, normalization metadata and research outputs to the public domain
under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Anyone may download, analyze and reuse these contributions for any purpose.
Source citations are retained for scientific provenance and reproducibility.

Third-party books, reports, catalogues, images and other source materials retain
their original terms. Their inclusion as sources does not relicense them under
CC0. The downloadable catalogue contains numerical records and source references;
it does not bundle the original modern reports, complete stellar catalogues or
image pixels. Consult the linked original sources for their terms and attribution.
