# Historical Mars catalog: coverage and limitations

The [catalog](../resources/catalog/README.md) includes a historical subset of **17,043 source
quantities in 5,374 groups** from Tycho Brahe’s _Thesaurus
Observationum_, _Opera Omnia_ X–XIII. The database includes 7,630 contextual
records and 50 diagram descriptions alongside the numerical material.

A quantity is one stated value: an angle, clock reading, correction, model input
or calculated result. A source group associates related quantities. Neither is
an independent-observation count. Repeated readings, summaries and calculated
positions retain separate roles.

See [catalog terminology](catalog-terms.md) for the distinct meanings of exact,
reviewed, verified and eligible. Region coverage is not a count of independently
image-audited numerals. Modern timing fields belong to the combined release.

## Pafko’s independent controls

All **923** workbook entries retain source links. Reconciliation records **906
magnitude agreements, sixteen reviewed differences and one incomplete printed
value**. Numerical agreement was not used to choose source identities. See the
[Pafko reconciliation](pafko-validation-status.md).

There are **123 recorded Mars declination entries without
workbook links**: 10: 12, 11: 34, 12: 73, 13: 4. This count
includes repeated readings. It is not a count of independent observations, newly
discovered measurements or proven omissions from Pafko’s selection. Adopted and
calculated declinations are counted separately by their source roles.

## Numerical interpretation

**16,984 quantities have exact numerical conversions; 11
are incomplete and 48 retain unsupported notation, components
or units.** Exact conversion preserves the stated number; it does not establish
measurement accuracy. Missing digits, denominators and unit hierarchies remain
unfilled. A source bound retains a threshold, not an exact measured position.

The 118 unclassified angular quantities retain known angular values without a
selected measurement type. Neither an expected trajectory nor a numerical fit
supplies their missing interpretation. Earlier interpretations remain available
when an explicitly evidenced unit, column or direction interpretation changes.
Original numerical expressions and components remain preserved.

The Latin S. in the XIII p.224 latitude tables is interpreted as
_septentrionalis_, north, supported by expanded Sept. labels in that volume.
This does not define English S. Elsewhere an unestablished hemisphere remains
null. [Observation methods](observation-methods.md) documents these scoped rules;
[edition findings](edition-findings.md) separates source variants, notation
questions, pipeline interpretation and actual edition discrepancies.

## Dates, clocks and corrections

The 1,083 date claims preserve source components and qualifications. The
Tycho subset currently has **no accepted absolute observing timestamps**. Calendar,
day origin, clock meridian and observing epoch require evidence. Contextually
completed clocks retain their printed components and source-hour references
separately; they do not acquire a modern time scale by conversion.

Recorded readings, adopted inputs, reductions, predictions and historical
corrections remain distinct. Printed corrections and witness alternatives do
not overwrite their originals. An unlabelled coordinate is not assumed raw;
an already reduced result must not receive the same correction again. Historical
parallax calculations are not independent parallax detections. See the
[correction comparison design](correction-comparisons.md).

Contextual locations distinguish reviewed book-page citations from provisional
automatic claims. Unknown physical pages remain unknown. An annotation’s
attachment page does not establish its full scope or physical printed location.
The [published books and scans](sources.md) supply the surrounding evidence.
